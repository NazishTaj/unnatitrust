About us

Unnati Educational and Charitable Trust

100% Fee Coverage India & Nepal Govt-Approved Colleges Only

Unnati Educational and Charitable Trust spreads the light of education and removes the darkness of ignorance from students' lives — guiding them away from uncertainty and towards a brighter, more secure future. Operating with the support of Rosemine Educational Trust, it is committed to ensuring financial difficulty never becomes a barrier: the chairman believes that even with limited resources, education should never be abandoned.

Chairman Mr. Samar Tanweer is dedicated to supporting students who are forced to discontinue their studies due to high college fees. His vision is to stand by such students and help them complete their education with dignity.

— Samar Tanweer, Chairman
Why We Exist

Our Objective

Unnati exists to make sure financially weak and deserving students never have to give up on higher education. Here's exactly what that means for you:

Scholarship, Not a Loan

Full tuition fee responsibility taken by the trust — nothing to repay, ever.

Simple Eligibility

Minimum 50% marks in Class 10 or 12 is all it takes to apply.

Building an Educated India

Every enrollment helps raise the country's Gross Enrollment Ratio.

Only Nominal Charges

You pay just accommodation & examination charges — nothing more.

No Scholarship Exam

Direct admission — stay cautious of fake scholarship tests.

Recognized Institutions Only

Admissions only in AICTE, PCI, NCI, NCBT, NBA or NAAC approved colleges.

Attention! Students of India and Nepal

Students from India and Nepal can apply for admission to the course of their choice in any state across India. The trust covers 100% of the tuition fees.
